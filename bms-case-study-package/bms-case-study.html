ACT IUnderstanding the choices

03Understanding the product, the evidence and the opportunity

What BookMyShow supports, what the research says, where the opportunity sits.

The existing product is capable and mature; this is not a claim that it is broken. Each group below keeps what is verified apart from what is inferred.

    What the current product already supports

    • Movie discovery includes language, genre and format filters.
    • The Delhi listing includes “Browse by Cinemas”.
    • Movie pages provide trailers, ratings / reviews and film information.
    • Release notes describe clearer separation of booking and billing details, and cancellation options on the order summary.

    Where this concept adds value

    • An optional natural-language planning path for people who start with an occasion.
    • Cinema search and comparable showtime trade-offs inside the booking flow.
    • Constraint-based seat combinations, with manual selection kept.
    • Exact booking, billing and cancellation information at a clear human review point.
    • Factual movie details: no redundant AI after a film is selected.

      04Reframing the problem

      Reframing the problem

      Movie booking connects several choices — what to watch, where to watch, when to go, what to pay and where to sit. The opportunity is to help people evaluate them with less effort while keeping booking understandable and under their control.

      How might weHow might we help moviegoers move from an initial intention to a confident booking decision by reducing the effort of discovering, comparing and selecting — while preserving their control over the final choices?
      DiscoverFrom an occasion to a short, explained list of films.
      CompareCinemas, times, formats and price, side by side.
      ChooseSeats and a final total the person verifies and authorises.

            08System architecture

            One orchestrator owns the task. Services own the facts.

            A single Booking Orchestrator keeps session state, calls bounded capabilities and trusted booking services, and asks the person before anything consequential.

            User request

            • Natural language or standard filters
            • City · date / time · party size
            • Budget · format · explicit preferences

            Booking Orchestrator

            • Maintains the booking-session state
            • Interprets intent, identifies missing constraints
            • Sequences approved tool calls
            • Delegates bounded tasks and reconciles results
            • Explains options; asks approval before commitment

            Human checkpoints

            • User picks film and showtime
            • User applies or rejects seat IDs
            • User reviews exact booking and billing
            • Explicit authorisation before payment

            The orchestrator delegates to four bounded capabilities

            LLM + ranking

            Discovery & preference

            Interprets outing intent, asks only for missing constraints, then ranks eligible films from catalogue metadata and preferences the user supplied or saved. Short grounded reasons; no assumed interests or match percentages.

            Tools + LLM summary

            Cinema search, showtime & value

            Searches by cinema name or locality and compares provider-returned showtimes, format, distance if available, listed price and fees. Explains trade-offs; never sets prices or claims savings.

            Deterministic rules

            Seat optimiser

            Solves adjacency, category price, party size and budget. Uses sightline only with reliable auditorium geometry. Returns feasible seat IDs; does not select, hold or reserve.

            Service lookups

            Status & recovery

            Checks authoritative payment and booking states, refreshes stale inventory and quotes, and separates pending, succeeded, failed and unknown. Never infers success from a timeout.

            Approved tools & sources of truth

            Movie catalogue & preferences

            Movie IDs, metadata, genres, languages, formats.Preferences only when provided or saved by opt-in.

            Cinema directory & showtime service

            Venue IDs, addresses, date / time / format.Returned availability only; no inferred inventory.

            Seat inventory & pricing / quote service

            Seat IDs, status, category and price.Fees, offer eligibility and itemised quote from services.

            Booking, payment & ticket services

            Validate quote, create booking after approval.Authoritative payment state, reference and ticket.
            NON-NEGOTIABLE BOUNDARYAI interprets, ranks, coordinates and explains. Deterministic rules enforce constraints. Authorised BMS / cinema / payment services verify live price, inventory, payment and confirmation. The user approves consequential actions.

            Conceptual interface contracts. They are not confirmed BookMyShow endpoints, API names or permissions. A trusted backend must validate IDs, permissions, quote version, idempotency and service responses; an LLM-generated “approved” flag is never transaction authorisation.

            ACT IIIExperiencing the solution

            09Experience in action

            Watch the booking happen. Then take over.

            A short guided film plays the selected flow once. Click, type or press Pause at any moment to take control. One illustrative scenario: a first date near Sector 3, Gandhinagar — Hindi, evening, two people, under ₹250 a ticket.

            Best experienced on a larger screen.

            The walkthrough follows the main path. Optional branches (returning user, filters) stay available through the screen numbers. All data is illustrative; nothing is booked or paid.

            What changes, and what does not

            Screens

            Both default walkthroughs are of similar length (Flow 1: 10 screens, Flow 2: 11). No saving in screens is claimed.

            Repeated entry

            Flow 1 captures language, time, budget and party size once, as a sentence. Flow 2 asks for them in separate controls.

            Manual comparison

            Flow 1 adds a short shortlist, a showtime summary and a seat suggestion. Flow 2 leaves all comparison to the person.

            Control

            In both flows the person picks the film and show, applies seats and authorises payment. No time or effort saving is measured or claimed.

            10AI capabilities & boundaries

            Four different jobs. Not all of them are an LLM.

            The LLM interprets and explains; ranking orders eligible options; the optimiser calculates seats; authorised services provide live facts; the user decides.

            SOURCE OF TRUTHLive catalogue / showtimes · seat-state service · final price / fees · eligible offers · payment status · booking confirmation / ticket.
            HUMAN AUTHORITYAI may interpret and recommend. The user decides. Authorised booking services confirm.

            PROPOSED CAPABILITY MODEL — this does not claim these agents, models or integrations are deployed by BookMyShow.

            11Beyond the happy path

            • What if a suggested seat becomes unavailable?
            • What if a quote changes just before payment?
            • What if a payment is pending and the confirmation is unclear?
            • How does the system preserve trust when the expected journey fails?
